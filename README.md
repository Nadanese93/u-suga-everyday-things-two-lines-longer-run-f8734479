Two lines explaining rain to a 10-year-old: a vivid analogy in line 1, and an honest account of what the analogy maps and leaves out in line 2. The program writes the entry to `entry.txt`.

> Rain is the sky watering its garden with a giant watering can, tipping slowly so every leaf and rooftop gets a drink.
> The can explains where the water comes from and why it falls gently, but it leaves out that nobody holds the handle, nobody aims it, and sometimes it pours too much.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
